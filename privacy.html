JOB AUTOFILL

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

What the extension processes

Job Autofill may process your resume, profile and contact details, job descriptions, page URLs, visible application questions and options, employment and education history, and answers you choose to save. Optional answers can include work authorization, legal disclosures, demographic identity, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, and criminal-history information.

Why it is used

The data is used only to prefill job-application forms, suggest truthful answers, attach your selected resume, and create application-history exports you enable. The extension does not sell data, use it for advertising, determine creditworthiness, or submit an application for you.

Where data goes

Your profile, resume cache, settings, and consent record are stored in your browser profile and optional Docker backend on your device. When cloud AI is enabled, the minimum relevant resume evidence, job description, page context, questions, options, and—only with separate consent—sensitive saved answers are sent to the AI provider configured in your local backend.

Control, retention, and deletion

Open Profile & settings → Privacy & Data to change or withdraw any optional permission. “Delete all local data” clears browser data and asks the local backend to delete its saved profile and resume. Files exported by the user remain under the user's control. AI-provider credentials configured separately in the companion backend, environment, or operating-system keychain must also be removed from that location by the user.

Security and contact

Cloud API keys stay in the local backend configuration and are not included in the extension package. Review every filled field before you submit. Privacy questions can be sent to zidiyaocanada@outlook.com.
