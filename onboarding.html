Data this extension can process

Your resume and profile may contain your name, address, phone, email, education, employment history, and social links. On job sites, the extension can read the page URL, job description, form labels, available choices, and your saved answers.

Optional saved answers can include gender, sexual orientation, disability, race or ethnicity, Indigenous identity, veteran status, work authorization, criminal-history answers, and other legal or employment disclosures.
